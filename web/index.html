Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is needed, and you can invite your whole team from day one.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings in a couple of clicks. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and there are no cancellation fees.

What team size is Tempo built for?

Tempo works best for teams of 3 to 50. Starter covers up to 3 people for free, and Team and Business have no limit on the number of people.

Can I export my data?

Anytime. Export your plans, tasks and Friday recaps as CSV or JSON from your workspace settings, on every plan, and for 30 days after you cancel.
